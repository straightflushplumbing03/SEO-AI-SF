## Internal-link conventions

- City pages link their 2–3 nearest neighbors (`links.py` builds this by proximity).
- Case-study pages (`/case-studies/<slug>`) link back to their city + service pages and
  to the `/case-studies/` hub; the hub is discoverable via the sitemap.
- No orphan pages: every generated page must have ≥1 inbound link (hub or sitemap entry).

## Generating pages that match gold markup byte-for-byte

`modules/objects/page.py` composes `head/nav/footer/page_hero/cta_band/faq_section/breadcrumbs`
from the same templates the site's build scripts use. When adding a page type, diff your output
against an existing city page's markup before shipping.
