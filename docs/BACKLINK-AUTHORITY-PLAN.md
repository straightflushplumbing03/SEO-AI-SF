# Backlink Authority Plan

Legitimate links only. The goal is genuine authority from organizations and
publications that already matter to Orange County homeowners, not manufactured
link volume.

## What we will NOT do

- Paid/spam link packages, PBNs, link farms, comment spam, automated link
  exchanges, irrelevant directories.
- Anything that trades a link for a guarantee of ranking.

## Types of organizations to pursue (in priority order)

1. **Local community / civic orgs** the business actually supports or participates in:
   - Local chambers of commerce (only if the company is/will be a member)
   - Community events, school/charity sponsorships (record as sponsorship, not link purchase)
2. **Industry legitimacy:**
   - CSLB license pages (link back from site once license verified)
   - PHCC / trade associations if a member
   - Manufacturer/dealer pages for equipment the company genuinely uses
3. **Local restoration/adjacent trade partners:**
   - Restoration companies, water-damage contractors, real-estate agents and
     property managers who've seen the company's work (ask for a link only after
     a real working relationship; never pay for it)
4. **Local / industry publications:**
   - Earned coverage: local news, community blogs, trade press — achieved via
     real expertise (e.g., "why is my water bill high in OC homes" contributor
     pieces) rather than paid placement
5. **Legitimate local directories:**
   - Only high-quality, human-curated directories that a real business belongs in
     (see LOCAL-CITATION-AUTHORITY.md Tier 2/3)

## Guardrails

- Never buy links.
- Never ask for reciprocal links as a condition of anything.
- No doorway/tiered link structures; no hidden links.
- If a link isn't obviously earned, don't pursue it.
