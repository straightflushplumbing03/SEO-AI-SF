## Known advantages Straight Flush already has

1. **Diagnose-first positioning** — site consistently explains *know before you
   drill/dig/open a wall*; this is a genuine differentiator vs. "we'll cut and
   look" plumbing.
2. **Real case studies** with honest Tier-1/Tier-2 distinction (guardrail: no
   fabricated jobs). Competitors using content-mill city pages can't match real
   documented jobs.
3. **Deep topical library** — 20+ academy articles on slab leaks, acoustic/
   thermal detection, copper pipes, hidden leaks (matches the brief's leak
   detection topical authority section).
4. **AI crawlers explicitly welcomed** in robots.txt (GPTBot, PerplexityBot,
   Claude, etc.) — few local plumbing sites do this.
5. **Clean technical base** — extensionless canonicals, sitemap, valid schema.
